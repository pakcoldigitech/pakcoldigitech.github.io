Badminton.

Features

Badminton is a racquet sport played using racquets to hit a shuttlecock across a net. Although it may be played with larger teams, the most common forms of the game are "singles" and "doubles".

Gameplay

Players score points by hitting the shuttlecock over the net and landing it in their opponent's court, or by forcing the opponent to make an error.

Rules

A game is played to 21 points, with a 2-point advantage required to win a game, and a match is won by the first player to win two out of three games.
