Cat Features

A cat is a domesticated member of the family Felidae, order Carnivora. It is a small carnivorous mammal with a finely molded head, supple low-slung body, long tail that aids in balance, and specialized teeth and claws that adapt them to a life of active hunting. The cat is the only domesticated species in the family Felidae and is commonly referred to as the domestic cat or house cat to distinguish it from the wild members of the family

Cat Facts

Superb Jumpers: Cats can jump up to six times their body length in one leap, thanks to their powerful hind legs. Whisker Sensitivity: A cat's whiskers aren't just for show—they help them sense their surroundings and detect objects, especially in the dark. Cat Communication: Adult cats usually only meow to communicate with humans, not with other cats. Healing Powers of Purring: A cat's purr has a frequency of 25-150 Hz, a range that can promote tissue healing and reduce stress in humans. 360° Hearing: Cats can rotate their ears 180° and have one of the best hearing ranges in the animal kingdom. Dreaming Cats: Like humans, cats experience REM (Rapid Eye Movement) sleep, the stage when dreaming occurs. Feline Longevity: The oldest known cat lived to an astonishing 38 years and 3 days old! Unique Nose Prints: Just like human fingerprints, each cat's nose print is unique. Fast Reflexes: A domestic cat can run at speeds of up to 30 mph (48 km/h) for short distances. Ancient Bond: Cats were domesticated over 9,000 years ago, with evidence suggesting they lived alongside humans in ancient Mesopotamia and Egypt. 🐱🐱🐱🐱🐱🐱🐱🐱🐱🐱🐱
