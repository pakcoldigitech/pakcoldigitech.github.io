solo leveling

  • Solo Leveling, also alternatively translated as Only I Level Up, is a South Korean portal fantasy web novel written by Chugong. It was serialized in Kakao's digital comic and fiction platform KakaoPage beginning on July 25, 2016, and was later published by D&C Media under their Papyrus label since November 4, 2016
  • Solo Leveling is an anime television series based on Chugong's South Korean web novel of the same name. It is produced by A-1 Pictures and directed by Shunsuke Nakashige, with Noboru Kimura writing the scripts, Tomoko Sudo designing the characters, and Hiroyuki Sawano composing the music.
  • Solo Leveling just aired its season 2 finale, and with that ending, of course viewers want to see more from Jin-woo and the rest of the cast as his power continues to endlessly grow. But in terms of season 3 of Solo Leveling? There is a surprising lack of real news, and what we do know is disappointing. Despite the massive success of Solo Leveling, crossing 700,000 reviews on Crunchyroll, the service’s most popular show ever, there has not been confirmation of season 3’s existence by anyone involved with the series.
