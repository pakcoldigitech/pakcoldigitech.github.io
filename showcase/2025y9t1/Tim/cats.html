Cats

New Zealand's national bird.

Features

Cats are fascinating creatures known for their elegance, agility, and independent nature. They have a sleek body structure, sharp claws, and keen senses, making them excellent hunters. With their diverse coat colors and patterns, cats come in a wide range of appearances, adding to their charm. These animals are highly curious and playful, yet they also value their downtime, often indulging in long naps. Known for their affectionate purring, cats form close bonds with their owners while maintaining their unique streak of independence.
