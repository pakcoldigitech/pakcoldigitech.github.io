About me

Hi! my name is Shastra, and my favourite movie is Love Don't Cost a Thing. I've loved it for as long as I can remember, ever since I was about three or four years old. I remember watching it for the very first time, After the movie finished, I asked my mum to play it for me again and again on repeat. It's one of those movies that has always been there for me growing up, and no matter how old I get, I'll never get tired of watching it. No matter what mood I'm in. if I'm happy, sad, stressed, or im just bored, I can always watch this movie. Watching it never fails to make me smile, and it's the one films I know I'll never get sick of.
