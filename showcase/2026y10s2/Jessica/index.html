Typewriter

The first typewriter was created by Christopher Latham Sholes. The first typewriter was invented in Italy. Christopher Latham Sholes was born on feburary 14 1819. Christopher was an american inventer and he created the typewriter and the keyboard.

Christopher Latham Sholes got inspired by the need to speed up typing and document writting. It was driven by a magazine article, a need to solve mechanical jamming, and earlier personal devices. Because Christopher Latham was a printer editor and a journalist he wanted a faster way of typing.

During the 1870's till the 1819's the Sholes and Glidden Typewriter sold for $125 each. In those days to make writting affordable for small businesses Index Typewriter were also introduced costing $5 to $40 each To compare the cost of the typewriter in the late 1800's $125 was valued roughly half an average workers annual salary making typewriters a luxurious office equipment rather than a normal consumer item. In todays value it is around $3000 each.

Due to the commercial of failure of earlier machines like Austin Burt's 1820's typographer the Christopher Sholes team were able to file a U.S Patent No.79265 in 1873. A patent is a legal document granted by a government that gives an intentor exclusive rights to their invention. Mr Sholes earned $12000 for his share of the typewriter patent when sold to Remington Arms Company. Today the original QWERTY design by Mr Sholes remains the global standard for computers and phones.
