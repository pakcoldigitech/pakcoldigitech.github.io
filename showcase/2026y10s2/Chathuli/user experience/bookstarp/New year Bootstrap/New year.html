New year

The Sinhala and Tamil New Year is a celebration of unity, gratitude, renewal, and cultural heritage, bringing people of all ages together to honour traditions while welcoming a year of peace, happiness, and prosperity

The Sri Lankan New Year, also known as Sinhala and Tamil New Year or Avurudu, is one of the most important cultural celebrations in Sri Lanka. It is celebrated every year in April, usually on the 13th and 14th, marking the end of the harvest season and the beginning of a new year according to the traditional solar calendar.

Unlike many New Year celebrations, the Sri Lankan New Year includes a series of auspicious times that guide important activities such as lighting the hearth, preparing the first meal, starting work, and exchanging gifts. Families gather to celebrate with traditional customs, religious observances, and festive meals

The festival is known for its vibrant traditions, including:

  • Preparing and sharing traditional sweets and festive foods.
  • Visiting temples and seeking blessings for the year ahead.
  • Exchanging gifts and wishing prosperity to family and friends.
  • Playing traditional outdoor games that bring communities together.
  • Wearing new clothes in colours considered auspicious for the year

Happy Sinhala and Tamil New Year! May the New Year bring you joy, good health, success, and abundant blessings
