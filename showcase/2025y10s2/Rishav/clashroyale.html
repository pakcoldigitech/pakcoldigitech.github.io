CLASH ROYALE CARDS

P.E.K.K.A

A heavily armored melee troop that deals massive damage to ground units. Slow but powerful.

Wizard

A ranged troop that launches fireballs to deal area damage, effective against swarms.

Hog Rider

Fast and aggressive, targets buildings with high damage and can jump over the river.

Mega Minion

A flying troop with solid damage and good survivability. Strong against air and ground units.

Miner

A sneaky troop that can dig underground and pop up anywhere in the arena.

Archers

A pair of ranged units with decent damage; good for dealing with air and ground troops.

Valkyrie

A tough melee fighter who spins with her axe, dealing area damage to nearby enemies.

Baby Dragon

A flying troop that breathes fire, effective against groups of troops with splash damage.

Goblin Barrel

A spell that throws three goblins directly onto enemy territory for surprise attacks.

Tesla

A hidden building that shocks enemies with electric bolts. Pops up when enemies approach.

Uce Gang

A spell that strikes the three highest hitpoint enemy troops or buildings with powerful bolts.

Ice Wizard

A chilled ranged troop that slows enemies while dealing splash damage with icy blasts.
