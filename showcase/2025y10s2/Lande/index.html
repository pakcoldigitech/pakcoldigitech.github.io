My EYES

How I See People

Everyone sees the world differently. For me, the most interesting part of it is how they act, what they hide, and what they care about. This site is my way of showing how I see people.

Why People Pretend

Sometimes people pretend to be okay even when they're not. I think they do it because they're scared to be judged, or they don't want to seem weak. It's sad, but I understand it. The world can be a tough place.

People Can Be Kind

I've seen people help others without wanting anything in return. A small act of kindness, like holding a door or giving someone a smile, can really change a day. These little things matter more than most people realise.

People Judge Too Fast

A lot of people make decisions about others before knowing anything about them. I think that's unfair, and it makes it harder for people to be themselves. If we just listened more, we'd probably understand each other better.
