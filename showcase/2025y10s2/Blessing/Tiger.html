The Tigers

Habitat and Distribution

Tigers are large wild cats that primarily live in various parts of Asia, including forests, grasslands, and mangrove swamps. They are most commonly found in countries like India, Russia, and Indonesia. Tigers prefer dense vegetation where they can stalk their prey stealthily.

Physical Characteristics

Tigers are known for their striking orange coat with black stripes, which helps camouflage them in their natural environment. They are the largest members of the cat family, with males sometimes weighing over 300 kilograms (660 pounds). Their powerful bodies and sharp claws make them formidable hunters.

Behavior and Diet

Tigers are solitary animals that mostly hunt at night. They primarily feed on large herbivores like deer, wild boar, and sometimes even water buffalo. Using their strength and stealth, tigers ambush their prey and are capable of running short distances at high speed to capture it.

Species of tigers

There are different types of tigers:

  • Bengal tigers live mainly in India and are the most numerous,
  • Siberian tigers are the largest and live in cold Russian forests,
  • Indochinese tigers are smaller and found in Southeast Asia,
  • Malayan tigers live in the Malay Peninsula and were identified in 2004,
  • Sumatran tigers are the smallest and live only on Sumatra island.
