Why New Zealand Needs Jollibee

Jollibee, the beloved Filipino fast-food giant known for its joyful branding and unique menu, has captured hearts across Asia, North America, the Middle East, and Europe. It’s time for New Zealand to be next.

Jollibee’s Signature Dishes

Chickenjoy

    Description: Jollibee’s world-famous fried chicken—crispy on the outside, tender and juicy on the inside. Served with a side of savory gravy.

    Why it’s iconic: Widely loved across the globe and a top favorite among customers.

Jolly Spaghetti

    Description: A uniquely Filipino-style spaghetti with sweet-style tomato sauce, slices of ham, ground meat, and hotdog slices. Topped with melted cheese.

    Why it’s iconic: Sweet, savory, and nostalgic—this dish is a childhood classic for many Filipinos.

Yumburger

    Description: A savory burger made with a 100% beef patty and Jollibee’s signature “Yum” dressing, served on a soft bun.

    Why it's iconic: Simple, flavorful, and part of Jollibee’s original lineup.

Burgersteak

    Description: Juicy burger patties served with flavorful mushroom gravy and steamed rice.

    Why it's iconic: Comfort food that blends fast food with Filipino-style rice meals.
