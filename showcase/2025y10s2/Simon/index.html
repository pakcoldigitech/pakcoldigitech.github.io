About the Club

Paris Saint-Germain (PSG) is a professional football club based in Paris, France. Founded in 1970, it has become one of the most successful and popular clubs in Europe.

  • Stadium: Parc des Princes
  • Founded: 1970
  • League: Ligue 1
  • Main colors: Blue, Red, and White
