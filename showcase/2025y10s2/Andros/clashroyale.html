Clash Royale

Clash Royale is a real-time, multiplayer strategy game developed by Supercell, combining elements of collectible card games, tower defense, and multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) gameplay. Players collect and upgrade cards featuring troops, spells, and buildings from the Clash Universe — characters originally from Clash of Clans and new ones unique to Clash Royale.

Troops

Troops are the backbone your deck in Clash Royale. Each troop card represents a unit that you deploy on the battlefield to attack enemy troops, buildings, or defend your side. Troops vary widely in attack type, movement speed, range, health, and elixir cost, making their roles in battles diverse and strategic.

Wizard

The Wizard is high-damaging troop character. He is a low cost (5 elixir) unit that is known for his spash damage and high DPS against swarms of troops. With his blue robe he shoots high-damaging fireballs from a distance which makes it devastating for low health point enemies.

Musketeer

The musketeer is a long range troop. Using her musket she fires lead balls at rapid speed dealing immense damage to her enemies. Because of her long range and fast firing she can counter many troops which makes her a worthy foe.

Hog Rider

The Hog Rider is a quick and speedy melee troop. He rides on his hog jumping over terrain and with his swift speed he can get to towers with ease. The Hog Rider is 4 elixir and is the perfect balance between speed and strength.

Buildings

In Clash Royale, buildings are a type of card that can be placed on the battlefield to serve various strategic purposes. Each building has unique attributes, and they generally fall into three main categories: defensive buildings, spawner buildings, and win-condition buildings.

Goblin Hut

The Goblin Hut is a Builiding Type. While enemy troops are in range of the tower the Goblin Hut will spawn Spear Goblins to throw their spears from a distance to their enemies.

Inferno Tower

The Inferno Tower is a Building Type. When enemies are close it blasts an inferno beam towards its enemies depleting their health in no time. The Inferno Tower can destroy swarms of troops because of its fast fire rate and quick health depletion.

Tombstone

The Tombstone is a Building Type. Once the Tombstone is placed down it will spawn 2 skeletons every 5 seconds. The Tombstone is an excellent defense as it distracts troops from gettin gto your towers.

Arenas

The different battlegrounds where your battles take place. Each arena represents a level range based on your player’s trophies, and they have distinct visual themes and unlock different cards as you progress.

Arena 1

Goblin Stadium is the gateway arena where players get their first real taste of competitive battles in Clash Royale. Its goblin-themed environment is charming and helps new players ease into the game while unlocking key cards that shape early strategies.

Arena 5

P.E.K.K.A’s Playhouse marks a major step up in Clash Royale gameplay with the introduction of the iconic P.E.K.K.A and Mini P.E.K.K.A cards. The industrial, mechanical theme fits the power and strength these troops bring. Players here start building more strategic and synergistic decks to climb higher arenas.

Arena 10

Hog Mountain is a dynamic and challenging arena where the pace picks up and strategic depth increases. The theme reflects a wild mountain fortress, and the Hog Rider card truly shines here as a favorite win condition. Players need to master timing, positioning, and deck synergy to succeed.
