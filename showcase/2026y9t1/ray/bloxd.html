Features

Bloxd.io is a free online multiplayer sandbox game that lets players build, explore, and compete in a blocky, voxel-style world similar to Minecraft. Released in 2021, it runs directly in a web browser, meaning no downloads are required, and it can be played on both PC and mobile devices. The game features a wide variety of modes—such as survival, creative building, parkour challenges, and PvP battles like Bedwars and SkyWars—giving players many different ways to play and interact with others. Its mix of creativity and competition, along with constant updates and community-driven content, has made Bloxd.io popular among fans of casual and sandbox-style games

pvp

You fight real players, not bots Use your mouse to attack (click) Movement (WASD + jumping) is super important Some modes include weapons or gear, others are more basic 🧠 Core PvP Skills 1. Aim & Timing Don’t spam clicks—time your hits Try to keep your crosshair on the opponent 2. Movement Jumping and strafing (moving side to side) makes you harder to hit Good players constantly move unpredictably 3. Positioning Stay on higher ground when possible Avoid getting surrounded 🏆 Beginner Tips to Win More Start fights when the enemy is distracted Practice in less crowded servers first Keep your distance if you're low on health Learn how knockback works (hit → step forward → hit again) 🔥 Advanced Tips Combo hits: Keep hitting without letting the enemy recover Strafe fighting: Circle your opponent while attacking Mind games: Fake movement to confuse enemies
