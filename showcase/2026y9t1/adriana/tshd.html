What is it about?

The netflix anime series, ‘The summer Hikaru died’ is a horror-mystery manga about two teenage boys, Yoshiki and Hikaru, who live in a small quiet rural village in the countryside isolated from everything. Until one summer Hikaru suddenly goes missing in the mountains. Months later he comes back, he looks the same, talks the same, and remembers everything- but Yoshiki senses something is very off about his best friend Hikaru.
