KATSEYE

KATSEYE is a global girl group based in Los Angeles.

Some of their songs are: Debut, Gameboy, Tonight I Might, Touch, Gnarly, Gabriela, I'm Pretty, My Way, MIA, Flame, Monster high fright song, Time Lapse, Internet Girl, and Mean Girls.

They were establised as a girl group in 2023 after doing a survival show for Dream Acadamy. You can watch the show on Netflix by the name of "Pop Star Acadamy"
