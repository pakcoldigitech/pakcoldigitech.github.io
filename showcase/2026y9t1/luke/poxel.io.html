What is poxel.io

Poxel.io is a fast-paced, browser-based first-person shooter (FPS) that has gained popularity for its high-skill movement and "pure skill" gameplay. Developed by Legion Platforms (the same team behind other popular .io titles like Kour.io), it blends the accessible nature of web games with the competitive mechanics of PC arena shooters.

Gameplay & Mechanics

Unlike many mobile shooters that rely on "auto-shoot" or heavy aim assist, Poxel.io is designed to be a "skill-first" game.Advanced Movement: The game features high-level movement mechanics including b-hopping (bunny hopping), air-strafing, sliding, and dashing. Skilled players can use "dash extending" or "rocket jumping" to traverse maps at high speeds.Gunplay: It features a low Time-to-Kill (TTK) and emphasizes precision. Headshots deal significant damage, and there is no "pay-to-win" element; all players have access to the same basic stats.Weapon Variety: Players can choose from assault rifles, SMGs, snipers, shotguns, and unique specials like crossbows or rocket launchers.

Key Features

Cross-Progression: You can play on a web browser (desktop or mobile) and sync your stats and skins with the Android app via a Google account.

  • Customization: The game has a deep progression system where players earn "PX coins" and gems to unlock over 1,500 cosmetics, including character skins, hats, and weapon wraps.
  • Low Latency: The developers use optimized netcode to ensure that hits register quickly, which is critical for a fast-paced FPS.
  • Map Variety: There are over 30 unique maps, ranging from tight corridors to vertical urban environments.

    • Game Modes

    There are 4 game modes:

    • Free-for-All (FFA): Every player for themselves.
    • Team Deathmatch (TDM): Two teams compete for the highest kill count.
    • Kill Confirmed (KC): Players must collect dog tags from fallen enemies to score points.
    • Domination: A tactical mode where teams fight to capture and hold specific points on the map.
