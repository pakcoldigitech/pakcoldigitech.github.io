f2 cars

Formula 2 (F2) cars are standardized, high-performance racing vehicles used in the FIA Formula 2 Championship, designed to prepare drivers for Formula 1. They are spec cars, meaning all teams use identical chassis, engines, and tires to emphasize driver skill. These cars produce over 600 horsepower, reach speeds up to 200 mph (335 km/h), and weigh 795 kg. Formula 2 Formula 2 +2
