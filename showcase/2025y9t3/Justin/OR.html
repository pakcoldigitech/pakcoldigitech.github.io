Olivia's Childhood

Olivia Rodrigo is a Philippino-American girl born on the 20th February 2003. Her career had a starting point when she was very young. She took singing lessons when she was 5, piano when she was 9, and started to write songs at 12 years old. Her first main acting role was in the Disney show Bizaardvark.

High School Musical

Olivia Rodrigo participated in the acting of High School Musical, in season 3, acting in 8 episodes. Fun fact: She was only 16 when she acted, which was quite young.

First Real Fame

Drivers License was a song in the album, SOUR. Some say Olivia's fame began when the song was released. Drivers License was a massive hit, breaking multiple records on release in 2021. The song even went to No.1 in some places around the world. Many say that this song was Olivia's first real fame, and it's still her biggest song to this day.

    GUTS

    GUTS is Olivia's 2nd album, released in September 2023. There are 12 songs in this album:

  • all-american b#tch
  • bad idea, right?
  • vampire
  • lacy
  • ballad of a homeschooled girl
  • making the bed
  • logical
  • get him back!
  • love is embarrasing
  • the grudge
  • pretty isn't pretty
  • teenage dream

    • This album was a major chart success, starting off as No.1 on the Billboard 200 in the US. The album also topped charts in 13 countries, including the UK, Australia, and Canada. All 12 tracks of the album were charted within the top 40 of the Billboard Hot 100, with the lead single, Vampire, charting at No.1.
    GUTS also became the fastest album to reach the No.1 spot in Apple Music in 80 different countries. It also became Spotify's most streamed album of 2023, and won Olivia 3 Grammys including best new artist.
