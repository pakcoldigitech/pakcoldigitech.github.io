Mini pekka

The Mini P.E.K.K.A. is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Training Camp (Tutorial). It spawns a fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee ground troop with high hitpoints and very high damage. He takes the form of a robot in dark gray metal armor that has two team-colored spikes and a helmet with a team-colored one eye and horns, while wielding a smaller version of the P.E.K.K.A.'s sword. A Mini P.E.K.K.A. card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.
