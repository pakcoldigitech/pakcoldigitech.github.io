Clash Royale is a fast-paced, real-time strategy game developed by Supercell that blends elements of collectible card games, tower defense, and multiplayer online battle arenas. Players build customized decks from a wide array of cards featuring iconic characters, spells, and defenses from the Clash of Clans universe—like Baby Dragons, Princes, and the fearsome P.E.K.K.A. The goal is to outsmart opponents in head-to-head battles by strategically deploying troops to destroy enemy towers and ultimately claim victory by toppling the King’s Tower. Since its global release in 2016, Clash Royale has captivated millions with its competitive gameplay, evolving arenas, and thriving esports scene

P.E.K.K.A is a heavily armored, slow-moving melee unit in Clash Royale known for her devastating single-target damage and massive hitpoints. Costing 7 Elixir to deploy, she excels at shutting down powerful enemy pushes and can dominate the battlefield when supported properly

mega knight

Mega Knight is a Legendary troop in Clash Royale, famous for his dramatic entrance that deals area damage upon deployment and his ability to leap between enemies, crushing swarms with ease. With high hitpoints and splash damage, he’s a powerful counter to glass-cannon troops and can turn the tide of battle when used strategically

Spells in Clash Royale are powerful cards that can dramatically shift the momentum of a match. They come in various forms—some deal direct damage like Fireball and Rocket, while others offer tactical advantages, such as Freeze to halt enemy movement or Rage to boost your troops' speed. With over 20 different spells available, players can choose from Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary types to suit their deck strategy. Whether you're clearing swarms with Arrows, resetting enemy troops with Zap, or cloning your army with Clone, mastering spell timing and placement is key to dominating the arena
