Aviation

Aviation is the broad and complex field encompassing all aspects of mechanical flight. At its core, it is the practice of designing, building, flying, and maintaining aircraft for a multitude of purposes. This includes the massive commercial airliners that transport millions of people and vast amounts of cargo across the globe daily, making international travel and commerce a cornerstone of the modern world. It also covers the specialized military aircraft used for defense and reconnaissance, as well as the diverse category of general aviation, which includes everything from small private planes and helicopters for personal use to aircraft used for flight training, agricultural work, and aerial surveying. The aviation industry is built on a foundation of rigorous engineering, intricate logistics, and a commitment to safety, with a highly skilled workforce of pilots, air traffic controllers, mechanics, and ground crew all working in unison to ensure the safe and efficient operation of a global network that connects people and places faster than ever before.
