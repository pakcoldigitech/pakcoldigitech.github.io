Ninjago

One of the best kids shows in the 2010s that still holds up.

Main Concept

The show consists of 6 ninjas:

  • Lloyd: The Green ninja.
  • Kai: The Fire ninja.
  • Cole: The Earth ninja.
  • Jay: The Lightning ninja.
  • Zane: The Ice ninja.
  • Nya: The Water ninja.

These six ninja have saved the world countless amount of times by big bads. The best ones they beated so far (in my opinion) is The great devourer, the Overlord, Lord Chen, Morro (Anti-Hero now), Harumi, Lord Garmadon(Anti-Hero now), The forbidden five, and Thunderfang. The last two are from their other show called Ninjago Dragons Rising.(More on that later.) So the ninja use their spinjitzu and their elemental powers to defeat the big bads with some friends along the way and they're awesome and comedic while doing it.

Ninjago Dragons rising.

Up until then, we knew the existence of other realms but Ninjago was the only thing in the map. After the merge happened, the Ninja went missing presummely for about 5-6 years all over the Merged lands. After that, there were these 2 teens, Arin and Sora, who were living in the crossroads by themselves doign Mech races when all of a sudden in the middle of a mech race, they had to save this baby dragon called Riyu from "The claws of Imperium." They succesfully saved that baby dragon with a little help from Lloyd.
