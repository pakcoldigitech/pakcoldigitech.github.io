Cats

Black cats

There are many cat colours and breeds. The most common colour you will see in a cat is the black cat. The black cat is known for their connection to witchcraft, halloween and misfortune. Theres a famous myth about black cats saying how they are witches in disguise and spy on humans in the night. A black cats' personality includes being independent, mysterious, and calm, with a preference for their own company and a slow, cautious approach to forming deep connections with others.

Orange cats

An orange cat is the craziest type of cat you will ever find. Why are they like this? Well, there's no scientific evidence directly linking orange coat color to personality traits like hyperactivity. However, a popular online stereotype and a 1995 study on riskier behavior by orange cats may contribute to the perception that they are more energetic or eccentric. Orange cats are mostly boys because the gene for the orange color is located on the X chromosome. Only 20% of orange cats are actually female.

Grey Cats

Grey cats are known for their intelligence and a balanced temperament, often being calm but playful and sociable with their families.
