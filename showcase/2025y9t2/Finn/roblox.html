When roblox was made

Roblox was officially released on September 1, 2006, with Roblox Studio being made available that same year. Games that were made by the community early in the platform's history included paintball games, haunted houses and model trains that players could ride.

Creator facts.

Roblox was co-founded by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel in 2004. Baszucki, also known by his former Roblox username "builderman," is a Canadian-born American entrepreneur and engineer. He and Cassel, who both previously worked at Knowledge Revolution, envisioned a platform where users could create and share their own games. Roblox officially launched in 2006.
