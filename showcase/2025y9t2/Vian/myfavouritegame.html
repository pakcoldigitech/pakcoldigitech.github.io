Vesteria

What it is

Vesteria is an online multiplayer game made in 2018 on Roblox by The Vesteria Team (Vesteria, Inc.). The lead developer Berezaa, known for other games such as Miner's Haven. The goal of this game is to explore and evolve in a fantasy world full of various inhabitants and unique areas.

When Vesteria was made

Vesteria was originally created on September 18 2018.

Stat Points

Stats are a vital mechanic of Vesteria, one of the core gameplay elements of the game itself. There are the Primary Stats of the game, that being STR, VIT, INT, and DEX. There are also the Secondary Stats which have 8 different stats. Stats points can be obtained in a few ways. Players can earn 3 stat points per level and 1 stat point per max level rebirth.
